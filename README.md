### 🧠 About me

- 🚀 **Co-Founder & Head of AI/ML at [Vezran](https://vezran.com)** — building **[Zyberpol](https://zyberpol.com)**, an agentic security-operations layer that doesn't just *spot* threats, it **closes them and proves it**.
- 🛡️ I work at the intersection of **AI and security** — agentic systems, LLM safety, prompt-injection robustness, and the evals that keep them honest.
- 🤖 Obsessed with the unglamorous 20%: **agent reliability, governed autonomy, and evidence you can hand a regulator** — not another impressive demo.
- 🧪 I build with the belief that the model is *rented and swappable*; the **control layer is the moat**.
- 📫 Reach me: **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/imranahamed)** · **[X @BeingImmu](https://x.com/BeingImmu)**
